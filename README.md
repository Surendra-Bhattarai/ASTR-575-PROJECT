# ASTR-575-PROJECT

Using secondary eclipse to find eccentricity, utilizing the the gap differences between the mid-transit and the two eclipses around the mid-transit.

WASP-18b showed clear signatures of secondary eclipses. We also did uncertainty analysis, and found that the parameters were more consistent with the literature values for non-zero eccentricity case even though it was less. We also considered the circular case, and it was clearly seen that the two gaps were almost same for ciruclar fit, while the gaps were slightly different for eccentric case, signalling towards the fact that we can apply such procedures to estimate ecc.

WASP-19b could not help us to come at a significant conclusion as it's secondary eclipse was not significant.

We further explored some of the poplular candiates like TOI-2119. It's secondary eclipse was visible clearly, but due to no clear presence of a 2nd secondary eclipse, the eccentricity estimation was not possible. But it was quite interesting to note the 48 hours shift in the secondary eclipse from the primary transit, while some chaotic nature of 2ns secondary eclipse on the right, but confident signature was not possible for the right one.
